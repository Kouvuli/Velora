# Defaults that make a page look generated

Adapted from Anthropic's `frontend-design` skill (anthropics/skills, Apache-2.0, `../licenses/anthropic.LICENSE`) and
Impeccable's craft floor (`skill/reference/craft-floor.md`, pbakaus/impeccable, Apache-2.0,
`../licenses/impeccable.LICENSE` and `../licenses/impeccable.NOTICE`). Condensed, merged and reworded for this
Skill OS; changed from the originals.

Every item below is legitimate for *some* brief. They are listed because they appear regardless of subject when
nobody decided. When the brief pins a look, follow it exactly, including any of these; when an axis is free, do
not spend it on one of them.

## Palettes and worlds
- Warm cream background with a high-contrast serif display and a terracotta / clay accent.
- Near-black background with one acid-green or vermilion accent.
- Broadsheet layout: hairline rules, zero radius, dense newspaper columns.
- Light or dark picked by product category instead of by where the product is used.

## Page scaffolds
- Content chopped into identical rounded cards (icon + heading + text) as the page structure; cards inside cards.
- The hero-metric template: big number, small label, supporting stats, gradient accent.
- An eyebrow / kicker label above every heading (Impeccable treats this as never earned).
- Section numbers 01 / 02 / 03 when the content is not a sequence.
- A modal for a task that needs neither interruption nor protected focus.

## Surface habits
- One radius and the same soft grey shadow on everything regardless of hierarchy; a 1 px border under a wide soft
  shadow (decide: border *or* shadow).
- Gradient text; gradient washes as decoration; glass and blur as decoration.
- A thick coloured left/right border on cards, callouts or alerts.
- Hard offset "neobrutalist" shadows in a design that is not neobrutalist.
- Sparklines, progress rings or rounded placeholder rectangles standing in for real content.
- Monospace as a costume for "technical" rather than for code, data or measurements.
- Emoji or Unicode glyphs instead of an icon set; icons of mixed stroke and weight.
- Decorative stripes or grid overlays with no canvas, map or blueprint under them; sketch-style fake
  illustrations.

## Typography and copy tells
- Accenting a single word of a headline in italic, bold or another colour.
- All-caps tracked labels everywhere; meta strings joined with middle dots; "WORD - fragment" labels; an arrow
  appended to every link and button.
- A system display face (Impact, Arial Black, the platform sans) as the display voice of a branded page.
- Copy that sells instead of explaining; "Submit" instead of the action; errors that apologise instead of saying
  how to recover.

## Motion
- The same fade-and-slide-up entrance on every section; hover transitions on every card. One orchestrated moment
  lands better; motion that answers a user's action is welcome.
